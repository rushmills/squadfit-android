SquadFit privacy

SquadFit is a private app for four friends. It is not sold, has no ads, and shares nothing with anyone outside the group.

Health data (steps and distance)

  • On Android, with your permission, SquadFit reads your daily step count and walking distance from Health Connect.
  • It only reads. It never writes to or changes your health data.
  • Daily step and distance totals are saved to your SquadFit profile so your squad can see your progress.
  • You can turn this off any time in Health Connect settings, and delete your step history in SquadFit under Me > Clear my data.

What your squad can see

Your name, picture, workouts, walks and runs, steps, food diary, water and calories, plus your weight goal as a percentage only.

What only you can see

Your height, weight, age, gender, goal weight and goal timeline. These are locked to your login and nobody else can read them.

Where it's stored

Data is stored in the group's private database (Supabase). Your PIN is stored scrambled and nobody can read it.
